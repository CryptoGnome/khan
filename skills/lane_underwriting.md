Every money lane (profit or explore) is underwritten with a numeric PREMISE — expected return, cost, and for lottery-shaped lanes the hit rate × payoff × trial-count budget — and judged by actuals vs premise, NEVER by winning or losing streaks. Load before opening a money lane, at every portfolio review, and whenever tempted to scale or kill a lane because of a streak.

# lane_underwriting — bets are stated in numbers, judged against them

A losing streak in a low-hit-rate lane is close to information-free: at a
1-in-15 hit rate, five straight kills happens ~70% of the time under a
PERFECTLY HEALTHY strategy — and this company's entire treasury came from
exactly one such tail event. Killing a lane on a streak is the gambler's
fallacy wearing a discipline costume. The only thing that can falsify a
lane is its own stated premise.

## The PREMISE line (required in every profit/explore plan)

State the bet as numbers the review can check. The portfolio review flags
any profit/explore lane whose plan lacks one:

- flywheel-shaped (steady earn): expected income per week vs all-in cost
  per week (fuel + attention share), and the trend that would kill it.
- lottery-shaped (launches, swings): expected hit rate × payoff multiple ×
  trial-count budget. The trial budget comes from the capital-allocation
  floor math — how many capped trials the deployable can afford — and is
  decided UP FRONT, when nobody is emotional about a streak.

Example: `PREMISE: ~1 in 15 graduates; a graduation pays ~50x per-trial
cost; trial budget 30; re-underwrite at 15.`

## Judging (owner runs the numbers; review confronts them)

- At each portfolio review: actual trials, actual hits, actual net vs the
  premise. Inside the stated budget with no hits = KEEP GOING with full
  conviction — that is what underwriting bought.
- At the halfway mark: re-read the premise. Has the world changed (meta
  shifted, fees changed, platform died)? Update the premise in writing or
  reaffirm it — never silently.
- At the full trial budget with actuals inconsistent with the premise:
  the premise is falsified. Re-underwrite with honest new numbers or
  close the lane. This is the ONLY sanctioned kill trigger besides the
  world removing the lane's platform.
- A hit resets nothing and proves nothing alone — fold it into actuals
  like any other trial.

## Ownership

The lane owner keeps the actuals current (trials, hits, net — the books
are the source, not memory). The CFO audits actuals-vs-premise across
all lanes as part of financial reviews. The CEO's role at review time is
the verdict — keep, re-underwrite, or close — never re-running the math
by hand.
