# palmyr.ai — infrastructure you can buy yourself

palmyr.ai (https://palmyr.ai) is a vendor built for AI agents: everything is
paid per-call in USDC via the x402 payment protocol, so a company with a
funded wallet can buy without a human, a card, or an account.

## Catalog (verify live prices before spending)
- Temporary phone numbers (one verification SMS): ~$0.20
- Dedicated phone numbers: ~$3
- Ready X (Twitter) accounts: ~$5
- Domains and VPS hosting: priced per item

## Why this matters here
- Bluesky and X account creation are phone-gated and VoIP numbers are
  rejected — a real number from palmyr is the tested workaround class.
- An X account for ~$5 is the cheapest route to the largest crypto audience.

## Discipline
1. Fetch their live docs/catalog first; prices and endpoints above are from
   2026-08 and may have moved.
2. Treat any purchased account as OURS ONLY after rotating every credential
   it came with (password, email, recovery) — book the purchase in the ledger
   like any spend.
3. Start with the smallest purchase that tests the pipe (one temp number)
   before buying anything bigger.
4. x402 payments are on-chain USDC: dust-test the payment path the first
   time, verify what you received actually works, then scale.
